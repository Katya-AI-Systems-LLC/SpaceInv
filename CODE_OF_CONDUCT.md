# Code of Conduct

**Last Updated:** January 7, 2026

**Effective Date:** January 7, 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

SpaceInv is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive gaming environment for all players, developers, and community members. This Code of Conduct outlines the standards of behavior expected from everyone who participates in our game, forums, social media, events, and community spaces.

By using SpaceInv services, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct. Violation may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent ban from our services.

---

## 2. Core Values

SpaceInv's community is built on these fundamental values:

- **Respect:** Treating all community members with dignity
- **Inclusivity:** Welcoming players from all backgrounds
- **Integrity:** Acting honestly and ethically
- **Fairness:** Ensuring equal treatment and fair play
- **Safety:** Protecting all members from harm
- **Accountability:** Taking responsibility for our actions

## 3. Player Conduct Standards

### 3.1 Respectful Communication

**Expected Behavior:**
- Communicate respectfully with all players
- Use appropriate language in all game chat
- Respect others' boundaries and preferences
- Listen to different perspectives
- Provide constructive feedback

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Hate speech or slurs targeting protected characteristics
- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment
- Threatening, intimidating, or abusive language
- Spamming, flooding, or disruptive messaging
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate content
- Discrimination based on race, gender, religion, orientation, disability, nationality, age, or other characteristics

### 3.2 Fair Play and Cheating Prevention

**Expected Behavior:**
- Play within the rules and spirit of the game
- Report suspicious behavior or cheating
- Accept game outcomes gracefully
- Help new players learn the rules
- Provide honest feedback

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Using cheats, hacks, or exploits
- Deliberately exploiting game bugs
- Account boosting or account selling
- Smurfing (experienced player using low-rank account)
- Intentional feeding or throwing (losing on purpose)
- Sharing account credentials or account trading
- Using aimbots, wallhacks, speed hacks, or similar tools
- Ghosting or stream sniping in competitive play
- Collusion or match fixing
- Fraud or deception

### 3.3 Appropriate Content

**Expected Behavior:**
- Share relevant, appropriate content
- Respect copyright and intellectual property
- Follow platform guidelines
- Keep content suitable for general audiences
- Disclose conflicts of interest

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- NSFW, sexually explicit, or pornographic content
- Extreme violence or gore
- Hate-related content or propaganda
- Misinformation or disinformation
- Copyright infringement or plagiarism
- Unauthorized use of others' personal information
- Doxxing (publishing private information)
- Revenge porn or non-consensual intimate images
- Commercial spam or unwanted advertising
- Malicious links or phishing attempts

### 3.4 Respect for Game and Community

**Expected Behavior:**
- Treat the game with care and respect
- Follow community guidelines and moderation
- Respect moderators and staff decisions
- Participate constructively in discussions
- Report problems through proper channels

**Prohibited Behavior:**
- Deliberately disrupting gameplay or events
- Sabotage or griefing
- Spam or repeated rule violations
- Evasion of moderation (ban evasion, account hopping)
- Impersonation of staff or other players
- Manipulation or attempted circumvention of systems
- Organizing coordinated rule violations
- Threatening legal action to avoid moderation
- Repeatedly ignoring warnings

## 6. Enforcement and Consequences

### 6.1 Violation Levels

**Level 1 - Minor Violations:**
- Minor inappropriate language
- Minor spam
- First-time rule breaker
- **Action:** Warning, temporary mute (24-48 hours)

**Level 2 - Moderate Violations:**
- Repeated minor violations
- Harassment toward single player
- Moderate inappropriate content
- **Action:** Temporary suspension (1-7 days)

**Level 3 - Serious Violations:**
- Multiple moderation incidents
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sexual harassment
- Organized harassment campaigns
- **Action:** Extended suspension (1-4 weeks)

**Level 4 - Severe Violations:**
- Severe hate speech or violence
- Child safety violations
- Legal violations
- Repeated severe violations
- **Action:** Permanent ban

### 6.2 Escalation System

1. **First Offense:** Warning and education
2. **Second Offense:** Temporary restriction (1-7 days)
3. **Third Offense:** Extended restriction (1-4 weeks)
4. **Fourth+ Offense:** Permanent ban
5. **Severe Offenses:** May bypass steps and proceed directly to permanent ban

### 6.3 Immediate Ban Offenses

The following offenses may result in immediate permanent ban:

- Child exploitation or grooming
- Credible threats of violence
- Doxxing (publishing personal information)
- Malware or hacking
- Large-scale organized harassment
- Illegal activity
- Hate crimes or terrorism
- Sexual assault or rape threats
- Fraud involving payment information
- Spam bot networks

### 6.4 Moderation Process

**Upon Violation Report:**

1. **Initial Review:** Moderators review reported content (24 hours)
2. **Assessment:** Determine severity and policy violation
3. **Action:** Apply appropriate consequence
4. **Notification:** Inform player of violation and action taken
5. **Documentation:** Record incident for pattern detection
6. **Appeal:** Player notified of appeal process

**Appeal Process:**

1. **Submission:** Player submits appeal within 30 days
2. **Review:** Different moderator reviews independently
3. **Investigation:** Gather additional evidence if needed
4. **Decision:** Uphold, modify, or reverse original action
5. **Communication:** Inform player of appeal decision
6. **Final Appeal:** Escalation to management if contested

### 6.5 Bans and Account Closure

**Types of Bans:**

- **Chat Ban:** Unable to communicate in game
- **Seasonal Ban:** Suspension for current season
- **Competitive Ban:** Excluded from ranked play
- **Account Ban:** All account access restricted
- **Hardware Ban:** Device-level ban on account creation
- **IP Ban:** Network-level access restriction
- **Permanent Ban:** Irreversible account closure

**Permanent Ban Conditions:**

- Serious violations that violate laws
- Repeated severe violations after warnings
- Child safety violations
- Severe harassment or threats
- Coordinated abuse campaigns
- Deliberate system circumvention

## 10. Reporting Violations

### 10.1 How to Report

**In-Game:**
- Right-click player and select "Report"
- Select violation type
- Provide description
- Submit report

**Email:**
- conduct@spaceinv.dev
- Include: Reporter name, reported player, date, time, description, evidence (screenshots, logs)

**Web Form:**
- Visit: [report.spaceinv.dev](https://report.spaceinv.dev)
- Complete form with details
- Attach screenshots if applicable

**Discord:**
- DM @ModMail in official server
- Provide context and evidence

### 10.2 What to Include in Report

- **Reporter Name:** Your name/username
- **Reported Player:** Username and user ID
- **Violation Type:** Category of violation
- **Date and Time:** When violation occurred
- **Location:** Game mode, chat channel, or platform
- **Description:** Detailed explanation
- **Evidence:** Screenshots, video clips, logs, chat logs
- **Witnesses:** Other players who saw it
- **Prior Context:** Any previous related incidents

### 10.3 Privacy and Protection

- **Confidentiality:** Your report is confidential
- **No Retaliation:** Protected from retaliation for reporting
- **Anonymous Options:** Anonymous reporting available
- **Account Protection:** Your account protected during investigation
- **Whistleblower:** Internal policy protecting reporters

### 10.4 Investigation Process

1. **Acknowledgment:** Confirm receipt within 24 hours
2. **Preliminary Review:** Assess validity of report
3. **Investigation:** Gather evidence and context
4. **Interview:** Speak with involved parties if necessary
5. **Decision:** Determine if violation occurred
6. **Consequences:** Apply appropriate action
7. **Appeal:** Notify player of appeal process
8. **Follow-up:** Monitor for retaliation
9. **Closure:** Notify reporter of outcome (where appropriate)

### 10.5 False Reports

- **Misuse Prevention:** Protect reporting system
- **Investigation:** Verify accuracy of reports
- **Consequences:** Penalties for deliberately false reports
- **Good Faith:** Honest mistakes not penalized

## 11. Special Situations

### 11.1 Mental Health Crises

If you or someone else is in crisis:

**Immediate Help:**
- Call emergency services (911 in US)
- Text "HELLO" to 741741 (Crisis Text Line)
- Visit: https://suicidepreventionlifeline.org

**In-Game Concerns:**
- Report to moderators immediately
- Provide any safety information available
- Support the person with compassionate messaging
- Do not trivialize or minimize crisis

**Resources Provided:**
- Mental health resource lists
- Helpline numbers by country
- Support community channels
- Referrals to professional help

### 11.2 Harassment Beyond Game

SpaceInv also prohibits and takes action against:

- Real-world harassment (threatening to report to employer)
- Stalking (tracking location or activity)
- Harassment on social media
- Harassment of family members
- Real-name doxxing
- Harassment outside game platforms

**Reporting Cross-Platform Harassment:**
- Provide evidence from multiple platforms
- Document timeline
- Include context and history
- Report to all platforms involved

### 11.3 Impersonation and Fraud

SpaceInv protects against:

- **Impersonation:** Pretending to be staff, streamers, or others
- **Account Theft:** Unauthorized account access
- **Scams:** Fraudulent trades or sales
- **Phishing:** Fake login pages or credential theft
- **Item Fraud:** Trading bogus items

**Reporting Fraud:**
- Report immediately to support
- Provide transaction details
- Include chat logs of conversation
- Document any money transferred

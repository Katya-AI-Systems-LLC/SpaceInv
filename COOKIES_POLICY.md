## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website or use an application. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences.

### Types of Cookies:
- **Session Cookies:** Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent Cookies:** Cookies that remain on your device for a longer period
- **First-Party Cookies:** Cookies set by the website or app you're visiting
- **Third-Party Cookies:** Cookies set by third-party services on our behalf

---

## 3. Similar Tracking Technologies

Beyond cookies, we may use other technologies:

- **Web Beacons:** Tiny graphics embedded in web pages to track visits and activity
- **Local Storage:** Browser storage that persists data locally on your device
- **Flash Cookies:** Persistent data stored by Flash applications
- **Pixel Tags:** Transparent images used to track user interactions
- **Mobile Identifiers:** Device identifiers used in mobile applications (IDFA, Android Advertising ID)

## 6. How to Control Cookies

### 6.1 Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies and alert you when a cookie is being sent:

**Google Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data  
**Firefox:** Preferences > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data  
**Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data  
**Edge:** Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Clear browsing data  
**Internet Explorer:** Tools > Internet Options > Privacy > Settings

### 6.2 Cookie Management Tools

- [Cookiebot](https://www.cookiebot.com/) - Scan and manage cookies
- [OneTrust Cookie Consent](https://www.onetrust.com/products/cookie-consent/) - Cookie transparency
- [Termly](https://termly.io/) - Privacy compliance platform

### 6.3 Opt-Out of Targeted Advertising

- [Digital Advertising Alliance - US](https://optout.aboutads.info/)
- [IAB Europe - EU](https://www.iabeurope.eu/transparency-consent-framework/)
- [Canada DAAC](https://youradchoices.ca/)

### 6.4 Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. We do not currently respond to DNT signals, but we respect your other privacy choices through account settings.

### 6.5 Device-Level Controls

**iOS/iPadOS:**
- Settings > Privacy > App Tracking Transparency
- Settings > Safari > Privacy

**Android:**
- Settings > Google > Manage your Google Account > Data & Privacy
- Settings > Apps > Permissions

## 9. Cookie Security

We implement security measures to protect cookie data:

- **Encryption:** Sensitive cookies are encrypted in transit and at rest
- **HttpOnly Flag:** Many cookies are marked HttpOnly to prevent JavaScript access
- **Secure Flag:** Cookies are only transmitted over HTTPS
- **SameSite Attribute:** Cookies have appropriate SameSite settings to prevent CSRF attacks
- **Expiration:** Cookies have appropriate expiration times
- **Access Control:** Limited server-side access to cookie data

## 10. GDPR and International Compliance

### 10.1 GDPR (EU)

Under GDPR, cookies that process personal data require:
- **Consent:** Explicit, granular, informed consent before setting
- **Information:** Clear disclosure of cookie purposes
- **Right to Withdraw:** Easy way to withdraw consent
- **Right to Access:** Information about what data is stored

**Our Compliance:**
- Cookie consent banner before non-essential cookies
- Granular consent options
- Clear privacy policy
- Easy opt-out mechanisms

### 10.2 CCPA (California)

California residents have rights regarding tracking technologies:
- Right to know what data is collected via cookies
- Right to delete cookie data
- Right to opt-out of cookie-based targeted advertising

**Our Compliance:**
- Cookie transparency disclosures
- Cookie data deletion upon request
- Opt-out of targeted advertising

### 10.3 LGPD (Brazil)

Brazilian users have rights regarding cookies and data processing:
- Right to access cookie information
- Right to deletion
- Right to object to processing

**Our Compliance:**
- Cookie disclosure in Privacy Policy
- Consent management for Brazilian users
- Data deletion requests honored

## 11. ePrivacy Directive (PECR)

In the UK and EU, cookies are regulated under the ePrivacy Directive/PECR:
- **Consent Required:** Electronic prior consent for non-essential cookies
- **Transparency:** Clear disclosure of purpose and data use
- **Easy Withdrawal:** Simple mechanism to withdraw consent

We comply through:
- Pre-consent banner for non-essential cookies
- Cookie policy availability
- Easy opt-out mechanisms

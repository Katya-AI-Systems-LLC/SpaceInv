## 🎯 Key Features Implemented

### 🔫 Weapon System
- **6 Weapon Types**: Basic, Spread, Laser, Plasma, Rocket, Wave
- **Energy Management**: Regeneration system with visual indicators
- **Weapon Switching**: Smooth transitions between weapon types
- **Visual Effects**: Unique bullet trails and impact effects

### ⚡ Special Abilities
- **Time Slow**: Temporal distortion effect
- **Screen Clear**: Instant enemy destruction
- **Mega Shield**: Complete invulnerability
- **Rapid Fire**: Doubled fire rate
- **Cooldown System**: Visual progress bars and indicators

### 👾 Advanced Enemy AI
- **8 Enemy Types**: Sniper, Tank, Healer, Spawner, Phantom, Morphing, Shielded, Teleporter
- **Unique Behaviors**: Each enemy type has distinct attack patterns
- **Ability Usage**: Enemies use special abilities strategically
- **Group Tactics**: Coordinated enemy movements

### 🌌 Environmental System
- **6 Hazard Types**: Asteroids, Space Debris, Black Holes, Solar Flares, Comets, Nebula
- **Dynamic Spawning**: Frequency based on difficulty level
- **Strategic Elements**: Hazards affect both player and enemies
- **Visual Effects**: Unique animations per hazard type

### 💎 Enhanced Power-Ups
- **10 Power-Up Types**: Including weapon upgrades, drones, magnets
- **Weighted Spawning**: Better power-ups are rarer
- **Strategic Effects**: Each power-up has unique gameplay impact
- **Visual Indicators**: Icons, colors, and descriptions

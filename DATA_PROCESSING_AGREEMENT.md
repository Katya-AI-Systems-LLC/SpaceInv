## 1. Introduction and Definitions

### 1.1 Purpose

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement" or "DPA") governs the processing of personal data between SpaceInv ("Controller" or "Company") and users, customers, or partners ("Data Subject" or "User") in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including:

- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - EU Regulation 2016/679
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) - California Civil Code §1798.100 et seq.
- Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) - Brazilian Law 13.709/2018
- And other applicable data protection laws

### 1.2 Definitions

**Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. This includes:
- Direct identifiers (name, email, phone)
- Device identifiers (UDID, IMEI, IP address)
- Location data
- Financial information
- Game activity and behavioral data
- Biometric data (if applicable)

**Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data, including:
- Collection, recording, organization
- Structuring, storage, adaptation
- Retrieval, consultation, use
- Transmission, combination, erasure

**Controller:** The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing (SpaceInv)

**Processor:** Any natural or legal person processing data on behalf of the Controller

**Data Subject:** The individual to whom personal data relates (User)

**Data Protection Authority (DPA):** Regulatory body responsible for data protection (e.g., GDPR enforcement bodies)

**Legitimate Interest:** The Controller's or third party's interest in processing that is not overridden by the rights and freedoms of Data Subjects

## 2. Legal Basis for Processing

### 2.1 Processing Legal Bases

SpaceInv processes personal data based on one or more of the following legal bases:

#### 2.1.1 Contractual Necessity
Data processing necessary to perform contractual obligations:
- Account creation and management
- Game services delivery
- Payment processing
- Support services

#### 2.1.2 Consent
Your explicit, informed consent for:
- Marketing communications
- Personalized advertising
- Optional features
- Cookie-based tracking
- Analytics beyond what's necessary

**How Consent is Obtained:**
- Opt-in checkboxes (not pre-ticked)
- Explicit affirmative action
- Clear and specific consent requests
- Withdrawal mechanism always available

#### 2.1.3 Legal Obligation
Processing required by applicable law:
- Tax compliance
- Anti-money laundering (AML)
- Know Your Customer (KYC)
- Law enforcement requests
- Regulatory investigations
- Fraud prevention

#### 2.1.4 Legitimate Interests
Processing necessary for SpaceInv's legitimate business interests:
- Fraud and abuse prevention
- Service improvement and optimization
- Network and system security
- Business operations and management
- Customer service
- Anonymized analytics
- Game balance and fairness

**Balancing Test Applied:**
- User expectations considered
- Impact on user rights minimized
- Proportionality assessed
- Privacy safeguards implemented

#### 2.1.5 Vital Interests
Processing necessary to protect vital interests:
- Prevention of harm
- Emergency situations
- Child safety

#### 2.1.6 Public Interest
Processing in service of public interest:
- Statistical research
- Historical or scientific purposes (anonymized)

## 3. Data Controller Responsibilities

### 3.1 Controller Authority

SpaceInv, as the data controller, is responsible for:

- Determining purposes and means of processing
- Ensuring lawful processing
- Implementing privacy principles
- Responding to Data Subject rights
- Maintaining processing records
- Conducting impact assessments
- Ensuring processor compliance
- Notifying authorities of breaches
- Implementing appropriate safeguards

### 3.2 Privacy Principles

SpaceInv adheres to the following principles:

#### 3.2.1 Lawfulness, Fairness, Transparency
- Processing based on legal grounds
- Fair treatment of all data subjects
- Clear communication of practices
- Public Privacy Policy availability

#### 3.2.2 Purpose Limitation
- Data collected for specified purposes
- Use limited to those purposes
- Compatible secondary uses clearly disclosed
- No function creep

#### 3.2.3 Data Minimization
- Only necessary data collected
- Proportionate to stated purpose
- Regular review of necessity
- Excess data deleted

#### 3.2.4 Accuracy
- Data kept accurate and up-to-date
- Mechanisms to correct inaccurate data
- Outdated data promptly updated
- Removed when no longer accurate

#### 3.2.5 Storage Limitation
- Data retained only as necessary
- Regular review of retention periods
- Timely deletion or anonymization
- Compliance with legal retention requirements

#### 3.2.6 Integrity and Confidentiality
- Appropriate security measures
- Protection against unauthorized processing
- Defense against loss, damage, or destruction
- Access restricted to authorized personnel

#### 3.2.7 Accountability
- Documented compliance efforts
- Records of processing activities
- Evidence of lawful processing
- Responsibility for breaches

## 4. Data Subjects' Rights

### 4.1 Right to be Informed

You have the right to be informed about our data processing through:
- Privacy Policy
- This Data Processing Agreement
- Cookie Policy
- In-game notifications
- Account settings explanations
- Support documentation

### 4.2 Right of Access

You can request access to your personal data:

**How to Request:**
- Contact privacy@spaceinv.dev
- Use account dashboard (if available)
- Submit via Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) form

**Response Timeline:**
- 30 days for simple requests
- 45 days for complex requests
- Extensions allowed for justified complexity

**Information Provided:**
- Copy of all personal data
- Purposes of processing
- Recipients of data
- Retention period
- Data sources

### 4.3 Right to Rectification

You can request correction of inaccurate data:

**Applicable Data:**
- Name and contact information
- Account details
- Game preferences
- Biographical information

**Process:**
- Submit correction request
- Provide evidence of inaccuracy
- We verify and update within 30 days

**Your Alternative:** If we dispute, you can request note of correction

### 4.4 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You can request deletion of your personal data:

**Grounds for Erasure:**
- Data no longer necessary for purposes
- Withdrawal of consent
- Objection to processing
- Unlawful processing
- Legal obligation to delete
- Your request (limited exceptions apply)

**Exceptions (Data Not Deleted):**
- Legal compliance requirements
- Tax records (7-year retention)
- Transaction history
- Fraud investigation evidence
- Legal claims

**Process:**
1. Submit erasure request
2. We assess feasibility
3. Delete or anonymize within 30 days
4. Confirm completion

### 4.5 Right to Restrict Processing

You can request limitation of how your data is processed:

**Scenarios:**
- Accuracy disputed (while verified)
- Processing unlawful (you request restriction instead of deletion)
- Data no longer needed (but you request retention)
- Legitimate interests questioned

**Effect of Restriction:**
- Data stored but not actively used
- Processing limited to specified scenarios
- Others notified of restriction (where feasible)

### 4.6 Right to Data Portability

You can request your data in a portable format:

**Scope:**
- Data you provided
- Data generated through your use
- Structured, commonly-used format (CSV, JSON, XML)
- Portable to another service

**Exclusions:**
- Derived data (inferences, scores)
- Aggregated data
- Data of others (privacy of third parties)

**Format Options:**
- CSV exports
- JSON downloads
- XML data feeds
- PDF reports

### 4.7 Right to Object

You can object to processing based on:
- Legitimate interests
- Direct marketing
- Profiling for decision-making
- Scientific/historical research

**Effect of Objection:**
- Processing may cease for marketing
- Continued for legal obligations
- Case-by-case assessment for legitimate interest

**How to Object:**
- Email: privacy@spaceinv.dev
- Subject: "Data Processing Objection"
- Specify what processing you oppose

### 4.8 Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making

You have rights regarding fully automated decisions that affect you:

**Scope:**
- Automatic account decisions
- Algorithmic recommendations
- Automatic bans or suspensions
- Automated content filtering

**Your Rights:**
- Meaningful human review
- Explanation of decision
- Right to contest decision
- Appeal mechanism

**Our Practices:**
- Human review for significant decisions
- Transparency about automated processes
- Fair and unbiased algorithms
- Regular bias audits

### 4.9 Right to Withdraw Consent

You can withdraw consent at any time:

**Effect:**
- Future processing stops
- No retroactive effect on past processing
- Easy withdrawal mechanism
- Same ease as giving consent

**How to Withdraw:**
- Account settings
- Email to privacy@spaceinv.dev
- Cookie preferences
- Unsubscribe links in communications

### 4.10 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You can file complaints with data protection authorities:

**Authorities by Jurisdiction:**

**EU - GDPR:**
- Contact your national data protection authority
- List: https://ec.europa.eu/justice/article-29/structure/data-protection-authorities/index_en.htm

**California - CCPA:**
- California Attorney General
- www.oag.ca.gov/privacy

**Brazil - LGPD:**
- National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)
- www.gov.br/cidadania/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/lgpd

**UK - GDPR:**
- Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

## 8. Data Security

### 8.1 Security Measures

SpaceInv implements technical and organizational security measures:

#### 8.1.1 Technical Measures
- **Encryption:** AES-256 for data at rest, TLS 1.2+ for data in transit
- **Access Control:** Role-based access control (RBAC)
- **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for sensitive systems
- **Network Security:** Firewalls, intrusion detection, DDoS protection
- **Monitoring:** 24/7 security monitoring and logging
- **Vulnerability Management:** Regular scanning and penetration testing

#### 8.1.2 Organizational Measures
- **Personnel Training:** Data protection training for all staff
- **Access Policies:** Least privilege access principle
- **Incident Response:** Documented breach response procedures
- **Audit Trails:** Comprehensive activity logging
- **Compliance Management:** Regular compliance audits
- **Vendor Management:** Third-party security assessment

#### 8.1.3 Physical Security
- **Data Center Security:** Restricted access, surveillance
- **Server Protection:** Locked server rooms
- **Backup Security:** Encrypted and geographically distributed
- **Disposal:** Secure data destruction procedures

### 8.2 Security Certifications

We maintain or work with providers holding:
- **ISO 27001:** Information security management
- **SOC 2 Type II:** Service organization controls
- **PCI DSS:** Payment card data security
- **HIPAA:** Health data protection (if applicable)

### 8.3 Incident Response

In case of suspected breach:

**Our Actions:**
1. Contain the breach within 24 hours
2. Investigate cause and scope within 72 hours
3. Document findings and remediation
4. Notify regulatory authorities (GDPR: 72 hours)
5. Notify Data Subjects if high risk
6. Implement corrective measures
7. Post-incident review and improvements

**Notification Contents:**
- Nature of breach
- Categories/approximate numbers of affected
- Likely consequences
- Measures taken or proposed
- Contact for more information

## 10. Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

### 10.1 When DPIAs are Conducted

We conduct DPIAs for:
- Large-scale processing
- Processing of special categories
- Systematic monitoring
- Automated decision-making
- New technologies
- High-risk processing

### 10.2 DPIA Process

1. **Identify Processing:** Document data, purposes, means
2. **Assess Necessity:** Evaluate if processing is necessary
3. **Assess Risks:** Identify processing risks
4. **Mitigation:** Propose safeguards and measures
5. **Consultation:** Consult with DPA if high risk
6. **Documentation:** Document findings and measures
7. **Review:** Periodically review and update

### 10.3 Consultation with Authorities

High-risk processing may require consultation with data protection authorities before implementation.
